## What Seity is

Seity is a **local, multilingual AI platform** for high-confidentiality institutional environments.

It works like the AI assistants you already know: it answers questions, analyzes documents, writes and reasons. The difference is that **it runs entirely on your own server. Your data never leaves.**

- **It runs on your server.** Every model (language, embedding, translation, reranking, vision and speech) runs on the organization's own hardware. No external API is ever called.
- **Nothing leaves your network.** Once installed, Seity needs no internet connection. Fully isolated (air-gapped) networks are supported.
- **Open and auditable.** Built on open-source components with an auditable codebase and no vendor lock-in.

|---|---|---|

| 10 | **Multilingual Support** | Query in one language, search and reply in another, fully offline |
| 11 | **Enterprise Access & REST API** | Multi-user, token-based auth, OpenAPI / Swagger, DMS integration |
| 12 | **Open Stack & Deployment** | Open-source components, optimized local inference, no lock-in |
| 13 | **Offline Maps & Geospatial Analysis** | *In development:* locally served map tiles, places and movements linked to sources |
